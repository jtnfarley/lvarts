Join us at Godfrey Daniels for an unforgettable evening of Open Mike Nights, NOW THE FIRST AND THIRD Sunday of the month! 

First Sunday of the Month Host: Andrew Dunn
Third Sunday of the Month Host: Bill Medei

The music starts at 7 pm, after a Scrabble tile draw, so arrive early to secure your place on the list to participate.  For those who arrive after the draw, don’t worry! We’ll still make sure to add you to the end of the list so you can share your talent with the audience.

In this unique listening room environment, Godfrey’s offers an atmosphere free from distractions typically found in bars or restaurants. Here, everyone is united by their shared passion for music. Join a vibrant community of artists who come together to play, listen, and support one another. It’s the perfect opportunity to network with fellow musicians and build connections within the local music scene.

Each set typically consists of three songs or lasts for 15 minutes. However, we may adjust it to two songs or 10 minutes to accommodate larger participant attendance on any given night. Regardless, you’ll have ample time to showcase your talent and captivate the audience.

Don’t miss out on this incredible community of music lovers at Godfrey Daniels Open Mike Nights. Mark your calendars and join us every first and third Sunday of the month for an evening filled with extraordinary performances, heartfelt connections, and a celebration of the love of music.
